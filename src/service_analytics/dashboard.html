04 / CONTEXT

Agency medians, ordered by request volume rather than speed. Hover for each eligible denominator.

@@resolution@@
Only closed requests with valid, nonnegative intervals are eligible. Unresolved requests are excluded; agencies with no eligible intervals are omitted. Closure times depend on request mix and cohort age. This is not an official performance ranking.

Data quality deserves a closer look

05 / QUALITY

Source inconsistencies are retained and made visible before interpretation.

@@flagged@@requests with at least one quality flag
@@quality@@
Flags may overlap: one request can trigger multiple rules. Flagged requests remain in the cohort; duration eligibility is evaluated separately. A flag identifies a case for investigation, not a proven cause.
Indicator definitions & interpretation
IndicatorDefinition
Total requestsIdentifiable requests in this creation cohort, including quality-flagged records.
Open / closedExplicit normalized source-status mapping at observation. Unknown statuses remain a separate group; they are not counted as open.
Median resolutionMedian source-local wall-clock hours from creation to closure for closed requests with valid timestamps and closure ≥ creation. Zero-hour intervals are valid. Group medians are never averaged into the cohort median.
Quality-flagged rate100 × requests with at least one implemented quality flag / all identifiable cohort requests.

The source is mutable. Results reflect the displayed observation, not historical status at the period end. Closure is not proof of satisfaction; unresolved cases are excluded from duration statistics. Elapsed wall-clock times near daylight-saving changes are approximate. No official SLA or causal conclusion is implied.
